# The smaller panels

![The default palette](images/palette.png)

## Session

The top row of the default palette.

- **Connection dot** — click to connect or retry. Hover for the current state and “Connected to Clip Studio Paint”.
- **Profile dropdown** — sets the document's color profile. See [Color profiles and gamut](Color-profiles-and-gamut.md).
- **Lock** — prevents layout changes.
- **Gear** — opens Settings.

Disconnect is in the row's **⋯** menu. Disconnect and Forget pairing are also in the tray and **Settings → General**.

## CSP Color

Clip Studio Paint's three slots: **Foreground**, **Background** and **Transparent**. Click a slot to choose where picks are written.

## Color Stat

The current color's swatch, **HEX**, channel values and **sRGB** appearance.

- Click the left half of HEX to copy.
- Click the right half to paste.
- Double-click to type a value.

## Color History

Recent colors, newest first, shown as tiles. Click a tile to reuse it. The list is stored locally in `history.json` and is not shared with Clip Studio Paint's palettes.
